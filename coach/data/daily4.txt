# daily · level 2 · useful verbs for conversation
admire|ಮೆಚ್ಚು|to respect and like|I admire her courage.
admit|ಒಪ್ಪಿಕೊ|to agree something is true|He admitted his mistake.
adjust|ಹೊಂದಿಕೊ / ಸರಿಹೊಂದಿಸು|to change a little to fit|It takes time to adjust to a new city.
afford|ಭರಿಸು|to have enough money for|I can't afford it right now.
announce|ಘೋಷಿಸು|to tell people officially|They announced the results today.
argue|ವಾದಿಸು|to disagree angrily|Stop arguing, both of you.
arrange|ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸು|to plan or put in order|I'll arrange a taxi for you.
assume|ಊಹಿಸು|to think something is true without proof|I assumed you were busy.
bother|ತೊಂದರೆ ಕೊಡು|to disturb|Sorry to bother you.
calm down|ಶಾಂತವಾಗು|to become less angry or worried|Calm down and tell me what happened.
care|ಕಾಳಜಿ ವಹಿಸು|to feel interest or concern|I care about you.
cheat|ಮೋಸ ಮಾಡು|to act dishonestly|Don't cheat in the exam.
cheer up|ಉತ್ಸಾಹಗೊಳಿಸು|to become or make happier|Cheer up! It's not so bad.
complete|ಪೂರ್ಣಗೊಳಿಸು|to finish|Complete the form.
consider|ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು|to think carefully about|I'll consider your offer.
continue|ಮುಂದುವರಿಸು|to keep doing|Please continue.
control|ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸು|to have power over|Control your anger.
correct|ಸರಿಪಡಿಸು|to make right|Please correct me if I'm wrong.
create|ಸೃಷ್ಟಿಸು|to make something new|She created a beautiful painting.
damage|ಹಾನಿ ಮಾಡು|to harm|The rain damaged the crops.
deny|ನಿರಾಕರಿಸು|to say something is not true|He denied taking the money.
destroy|ನಾಶಮಾಡು|to damage completely|The fire destroyed the shop.
develop|ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿಪಡಿಸು|to grow or make better|She developed a new skill.
disappear|ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು|to go out of sight|The sun disappeared behind the clouds.
disturb|ತೊಂದರೆ ಕೊಡು|to interrupt|Please don't disturb me, I'm studying.
doubt|ಸಂದೇಹಿಸು|to feel unsure|I doubt he'll come on time.
encourage|ಪ್ರೋತ್ಸಾಹಿಸು|to give confidence|My teacher encouraged me to speak.
exaggerate|ಉತ್ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಿಸು|to make something sound bigger than it is|Don't exaggerate, it wasn't that bad.
exchange|ವಿನಿಮಯ ಮಾಡು|to give and get back something|Can I exchange this shirt?
exercise|ವ್ಯಾಯಾಮ ಮಾಡು|to move your body to stay healthy|I exercise for thirty minutes daily.
explore|ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸು|to travel to discover|Let's explore the old town.
express|ವ್ಯಕ್ತಪಡಿಸು|to show a feeling or idea|It's hard to express it in English.
fail|ವಿಫಲವಾಗು|to not succeed|Don't be afraid to fail.
fit|ಸರಿಹೊಂದು|to be the right size|These shoes don't fit me.
gain|ಗಳಿಸು|to get more of|I gained two kilos this month.
greet|ಶುಭಾಶಯ ಹೇಳು|to say hello|She greeted us with a smile.
guide|ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶನ ನೀಡು|to show the way|Can you guide me to the station?
ignore|ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷಿಸು|to pay no attention|Just ignore him.
imagine|ಕಲ್ಪಿಸಿಕೊ|to picture in your mind|Imagine you are on a beach.
include|ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು|to have as part of|The price includes breakfast.
increase|ಹೆಚ್ಚಿಸು|to make bigger|Prices increase every year.
reduce|ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡು|to make smaller|Reduce sugar in your diet.
inform|ತಿಳಿಸು|to tell|Please inform me if you're late.
insist|ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸು|to say firmly|She insisted on paying.
interrupt|ಅಡ್ಡಿಪಡಿಸು|to stop someone while speaking|Sorry to interrupt, but it's urgent.
introduce|ಪರಿಚಯಿಸು|to make someone known|Let me introduce my friend.
joke|ತಮಾಷೆ ಮಾಡು|to say funny things|I'm only joking.
judge|ತೀರ್ಪು ನೀಡು|to form an opinion|Don't judge people by their clothes.
last|ಉಳಿ / ಬಾಳು|to continue for a time|How long will the meeting last?
lead|ಮುನ್ನಡೆಸು|to guide or be in front|She leads the team.
mean|ಅರ್ಥ ಕೊಡು|to have as meaning|What does this word mean?
measure|ಅಳೆ|to find the size|Measure the window.
mind|ಅಭ್ಯಂತರ ಪಡು|to be bothered by|Do you mind if I sit here?
miss|ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿಕೊ|to fail to catch or attend|I missed the class.
obey|ಪಾಲಿಸು|to do what you are told|Obey the traffic rules.
overcome|ಜಯಿಸು|to succeed against a problem|She overcame her fear of speaking.
pretend|ನಟಿಸು|to act as if something is true|He pretended to be asleep.
prevent|ತಡೆ|to stop something from happening|Washing hands prevents infection.
protect|ರಕ್ಷಿಸು|to keep safe|Sunscreen protects your skin.
prove|ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು|to show something is true|Can you prove it?
provide|ಒದಗಿಸು|to give|The hotel provides free breakfast.
quit|ಬಿಡು|to stop doing|He quit smoking.
recognise|ಗುರುತಿಸು|to know someone you have seen before|I didn't recognise you!
recommend|ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು|to suggest as good|Can you recommend a good dentist?
refuse|ನಿರಾಕರಿಸು|to say no|She refused to help.
regret|ವಿಷಾದಿಸು|to feel sorry about something you did|I regret not studying harder.
relax|ವಿಶ್ರಮಿಸು|to rest and become calm|Sit down and relax.
remove|ತೆಗೆದುಹಾಕು|to take away|Remove your shoes before entering.
repair|ದುರಸ್ತಿ ಮಾಡು|to fix|The mechanic repaired my bike.
replace|ಬದಲಿಸು|to put something new in place|Replace the old bulb.
request|ವಿನಂತಿಸು|to ask politely|I request you to sign here.
respect|ಗೌರವಿಸು|to admire and value|Respect your elders.
risk|ಅಪಾಯಕ್ಕೆ ಒಡ್ಡು|to do something that may be dangerous|Don't risk your health.
rush|ಆತುರಪಡು|to hurry|There's no need to rush.
satisfy|ತೃಪ್ತಿಪಡಿಸು|to please|The answer didn't satisfy him.
scold|ಬೈಯ್ಯು|to speak angrily to someone|My mother scolded me for coming late.
seem|ತೋರು|to appear to be|You seem tired.
settle|ನೆಲೆಸು / ಬಗೆಹರಿಸು|to make a home; to solve|They settled in Bengaluru.
shake|ಅಲುಗಾಡಿಸು|to move quickly up and down|Shake the bottle before use.
shift|ಸ್ಥಳಾಂತರಿಸು|to move|We shifted to a new flat.
shine|ಹೊಳೆ|to give bright light|The stars shine at night.
solve|ಪರಿಹರಿಸು|to find an answer|Let's solve this problem together.
spread|ಹರಡು|to go over a large area|The news spread quickly.
struggle|ಹೆಣಗಾಡು|to try hard with difficulty|I struggle with English grammar.
succeed|ಯಶಸ್ವಿಯಾಗು|to do well|If you work hard, you'll succeed.
suffer|ನರಳು|to feel pain or trouble|He suffers from back pain.
supply|ಪೂರೈಸು|to provide|The shop supplies medicines to hospitals.
support|ಬೆಂಬಲಿಸು|to help and encourage|My family supports me.
surprise|ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯಪಡಿಸು|to cause sudden wonder|Let's surprise her on her birthday.
survive|ಬದುಕುಳಿ|to stay alive|Everyone survived the accident.
suspect|ಶಂಕಿಸು|to think something is probably true|I suspect he forgot.
tease|ಕೆಣಕು|to make fun of someone|Don't tease your sister.
tolerate|ಸಹಿಸು|to accept something you don't like|I can't tolerate rudeness.
treat|ವರ್ತಿಸು / ಉಪಚರಿಸು|to behave towards; to give medical care|Treat everyone with respect.
upset|ಬೇಸರಪಡಿಸು|to make unhappy|His words upset me.
volunteer|ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವೆ ಮಾಡು|to offer to help without pay|I volunteer at an old-age home.
warn|ಎಚ್ಚರಿಸು|to tell about danger|I warned you about the dog.
waste|ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡು|to use badly|Don't waste food.
wonder|ಯೋಚಿಸು / ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯಪಡು|to want to know|I wonder why she left.
get along|ಹೊಂದಿಕೊಂಡು ಹೋಗು|to have a friendly relationship|I get along well with my colleagues.
give up|ಬಿಟ್ಟುಕೊಡು|to stop trying|Never give up.
look after|ನೋಡಿಕೊ|to take care of|She looks after her parents.

look forward to|ಎದುರುನೋಡು|to wait happily for|I'm looking forward to the holidays.
find out|ತಿಳಿದುಕೊ|to discover information|Let me find out the train timings.
pick up|ಕರೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಬಾ / ಎತ್ತಿಕೊ|to collect someone; to lift|I'll pick you up at seven.

run out of|ಖಾಲಿಯಾಗು|to have no more|We've run out of milk.
set up|ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು|to arrange or start|She set up her own clinic.
take care|ಜೋಪಾನವಾಗಿರು|to be careful|Take care on the road.

calm|ಸಮಾಧಾನ ಪಡಿಸು|to make someone less upset|She calmed the crying child.
