dengue|ಡೆಂಗ್ಯೂ|a viral fever spread by mosquitoes|Platelets fall in dengue.

measles|ದಡಾರ|a viral illness with rash and fever|Measles can be prevented by vaccine.
infection|ಸೋಂಕು|disease caused by germs|The wound shows signs of infection.
inflammation|ಉರಿಯೂತ|redness, swelling, heat and pain|There's inflammation in the joint.
allergy|ಅಲರ್ಜಿ|a reaction to something harmless|She has a peanut allergy.
arthritis|ಸಂಧಿವಾತ|pain and swelling in joints|Arthritis is common in old age.
obesity|ಸ್ಥೂಲಕಾಯ|being very overweight|Obesity increases the risk of diabetes.

stroke|ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು|sudden loss of blood to the brain|Facial weakness can be a sign of stroke.
paralysis|ಲಕ್ವ|loss of movement|The stroke caused paralysis of the left side.
epilepsy|ಅಪಸ್ಮಾರ|a condition with repeated fits|He's on medicine for epilepsy.
cancer|ಕ್ಯಾನ್ಸರ್|uncontrolled growth of cells|Early detection of cancer saves lives.
tumour|ಗೆಡ್ಡೆ|an abnormal mass of tissue|The tumour is not cancerous.
benign|ಹಾನಿಕಾರಕವಲ್ಲದ|not cancerous|Good news, the lump is benign.
malignant|ಮಾರಕ|cancerous|The biopsy shows a malignant growth.
ulcer|ಹುಣ್ಣು|an open sore|He has a stomach ulcer.
kidney stone|ಮೂತ್ರಪಿಂಡದ ಕಲ್ಲು|a hard deposit in the kidney|Kidney stones cause severe pain.
appendicitis|ಅಪೆಂಡಿಸೈಟಿಸ್|inflammation of the appendix|Pain in the lower right abdomen may be appendicitis.
gastritis|ಜಠರದ ಉರಿಯೂತ|inflammation of the stomach lining|Gastritis causes burning pain.
dehydration|ನಿರ್ಜಲೀಕರಣ|not enough water in the body|Loose motions can cause dehydration.
sunstroke|ಬಿಸಿಲ ಆಘಾತ|illness from too much heat|Stay indoors to avoid sunstroke.
food poisoning|ಆಹಾರ ವಿಷಬಾಧೆ|illness from bad food|The whole family had food poisoning.
thyroid problem|ಥೈರಾಯ್ಡ್ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ|a problem with the thyroid gland|She takes a tablet for her thyroid.
cholesterol|ಕೊಲೆಸ್ಟ್ರಾಲ್|a fat in blood|High cholesterol can block arteries.
depression|ಖಿನ್ನತೆ|a long-lasting sadness and low mood|Depression is an illness, not weakness.
anxiety|ಆತಂಕ|strong worry and fear|Anxiety can cause palpitations.
insomnia|ನಿದ್ರಾಹೀನತೆ|difficulty sleeping|Stress can cause insomnia.
pregnancy|ಗರ್ಭಾವಸ್ಥೆ|carrying a baby in the womb|She's in the seventh month of pregnancy.
pregnant|ಗರ್ಭಿಣಿ|carrying a baby|Are you pregnant?
delivery|ಹೆರಿಗೆ|giving birth|It was a normal delivery.
caesarean|ಸಿಸೇರಿಯನ್|delivery by operation|She had a caesarean section.
miscarriage|ಗರ್ಭಪಾತ|loss of pregnancy early|She had a miscarriage last year.
period|ಮುಟ್ಟು|monthly bleeding in women|When was your last period?
menstrual cramps|ಮುಟ್ಟಿನ ನೋವು|pain during periods|She gets bad menstrual cramps.
breastfeeding|ಎದೆಹಾಲುಣಿಸುವಿಕೆ|feeding a baby with breast milk|Breastfeeding is best for babies.
newborn|ನವಜಾತ ಶಿಶು|a baby just born|The newborn weighs three kilos.
premature|ಅವಧಿಪೂರ್ವ|born too early|The baby was premature.
growth|ಬೆಳವಣಿಗೆ|getting bigger|The child's growth is normal.
malnutrition|ಅಪೌಷ್ಟಿಕತೆ|poor nutrition|Malnutrition is still common in villages.
vitamin deficiency|ಜೀವಸತ್ವದ ಕೊರತೆ|not enough vitamins|Vitamin D deficiency is very common.
elderly|ವೃದ್ಧರು|old people|Elderly patients need extra care.
disability|ಅಂಗವೈಕಲ್ಯ|a condition that limits movement or senses|Make the clinic accessible for people with disabilities.
blind|ಅಂಧ|not able to see|He's blind in one eye.
deaf|ಕಿವುಡ|not able to hear|Speak slowly; she's a little deaf.
addiction|ವ್ಯಸನ|not able to stop using something harmful|He needs help for his alcohol addiction.
smoking|ಧೂಮಪಾನ|breathing in tobacco smoke|Smoking causes lung cancer.
alcohol|ಮದ್ಯ|drinks like beer and whisky|Do you drink alcohol?
snakebite|ಹಾವು ಕಡಿತ|a bite from a snake|Bring the snakebite patient quickly.
dog bite|ನಾಯಿ ಕಡಿತ|a bite from a dog|After a dog bite, take the rabies vaccine.
poisoning|ವಿಷಬಾಧೆ|harm from swallowing poison|Pesticide poisoning is an emergency.
accident|ಅಪಘಾತ|a sudden harmful event|He was injured in a road accident.
