# study · level 3 · learning skills and student life
active learning|ಸಕ್ರಿಯ ಕಲಿಕೆ|learning by doing and discussing|Active learning helps memory.
retain|ಉಳಿಸಿಕೊ (ನೆನಪಿನಲ್ಲಿ)|to keep in memory|Teaching others helps you retain information.

self-test|ಸ್ವಯಂ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ|testing yourself|Self-test after every chapter.
weak area|ದುರ್ಬಲ ವಿಷಯ|a topic you don't know well|Focus on your weak areas.
strong area|ಬಲವಾದ ವಿಷಯ|a topic you know well|Biochemistry is my strong area.
target score|ಗುರಿ ಅಂಕ|the marks you aim for|My target score is 600.
schedule|ವೇಳಾಪಟ್ಟಿ|plan of time|Stick to your study schedule.
break time|ವಿರಾಮದ ಸಮಯ|time to rest|Take a five-minute break every hour.
distraction|ಗಮನಭಂಗ|something that takes your attention|Phones are the biggest distraction.
quiet place|ಶಾಂತ ಸ್ಥಳ|a place without noise|Find a quiet place to study.
well-rested|ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ ಪಡೆದ|having had enough sleep|Go to the exam well-rested.
last-minute|ಕೊನೆಯ ಕ್ಷಣದ|at the very end|Avoid last-minute studying.
cramming|ತುರುಕುವಿಕೆ|studying a lot in a short time|Cramming doesn't help long-term memory.
understand deeply|ಆಳವಾಗಿ ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೊ|to know the reason behind facts|Understand deeply; don't just memorise.
apply|ಅನ್ವಯಿಸು|to use knowledge in practice|Apply what you learn on patients.
connect ideas|ಆಲೋಚನೆಗಳನ್ನು ಜೋಡಿಸು|to link topics together|Connect anatomy with physiology.
mind map|ಮನೋನಕ್ಷೆ|a diagram of linked ideas|Make a mind map of the chapter.
outline|ರೂಪುರೇಷೆ|main points|Write an outline before the essay.
handwriting|ಕೈಬರಹ|the way you write by hand|Neat handwriting gets better marks.
legible|ಓದಲು ಸಾಧ್ಯವಾದ|easy to read|Write legibly.
spelling mistake|ಕಾಗುಣಿತ ದೋಷ|a word written wrongly|Check for spelling mistakes.
proofread|ತಿದ್ದಿ ಓದು|to read to find errors|Proofread your thesis.
word limit|ಪದಮಿತಿ|maximum number of words|The word limit is 500.
assignment deadline|ನಿಯೋಜನೆಯ ಗಡುವು|last date for submission|The assignment deadline is Friday.
extension request|ಗಡುವು ವಿಸ್ತರಣೆ ವಿನಂತಿ|asking for more time|I sent an extension request.
