# work · level 2 · emails, calls and meetings (useful expressions)
dear sir or madam|ಮಾನ್ಯರೇ|a formal greeting in letters|Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to apply for the post.
regarding|ಕುರಿತು|about|I'm writing regarding my leave request.
kindly|ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು|please (formal)|Kindly send the documents.
as discussed|ಚರ್ಚಿಸಿದಂತೆ|as we talked about|As discussed, I'm sending the report.
please find attached|ಲಗತ್ತಿಸಲಾಗಿದೆ|the file is added to this email|Please find attached my resume.
looking forward to hearing from you|ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯೆಗಾಗಿ ಕಾಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ|I'm waiting for your reply|Looking forward to hearing from you.
best regards|ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳೊಂದಿಗೆ|a polite ending for emails|Best regards, Dr. Priya.
for your information|ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮಾಹಿತಿಗಾಗಿ|just to let you know|For your information, the lab closes at six.
at the earliest|ಆದಷ್ಟು ಬೇಗ|as soon as possible|Please reply at the earliest.
gentle reminder|ಸೌಮ್ಯ ಜ್ಞಾಪನೆ|a polite reminder|This is a gentle reminder about the meeting.
noted|ಗಮನಿಸಲಾಗಿದೆ|I have read and understood|"The meeting is at 3." "Noted, thank you."
thanks in advance|ಮುಂಚಿತವಾಗಿ ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು|thank you before they help|Thanks in advance for your help.
I'd like to|ನಾನು … ಬಯಸುತ್ತೇನೆ|polite way to say "I want to"|I'd like to discuss my schedule.
could you please|ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು … ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀರಾ|a polite request|Could you please check this?
would it be possible|… ಸಾಧ್ಯವೇ|a very polite request|Would it be possible to change my shift?
I'm afraid|ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, … ಎಂದು ಹೇಳಲು ಬೇಸರ|polite way to give bad news|I'm afraid I can't attend.
let me check|ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ ಹೇಳುತ್ತೇನೆ|I'll find out|Let me check and get back to you.
get back to you|ನಿಮಗೆ ಮತ್ತೆ ತಿಳಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ|to reply later|I'll get back to you by evening.
on behalf of|ಪರವಾಗಿ|for someone else|I'm calling on behalf of Dr. Rao.
put you through|ಸಂಪರ್ಕ ಕಲ್ಪಿಸು (ಕರೆ)|to connect your call|Please hold, I'll put you through.
hold the line|ಕರೆಯಲ್ಲಿರಿ|wait on the phone|Please hold the line.
speaking|ಮಾತನಾಡುತ್ತಿರುವುದು (ನಾನೇ)|said when someone asks for you on the phone|"Can I speak to Anita?" "Speaking."
wrong number|ತಪ್ಪು ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ|a call to the wrong person|Sorry, wrong number.
call me back|ಮರಳಿ ಕರೆ ಮಾಡಿ|phone me again|Please call me back when you're free.
leave a message|ಸಂದೇಶ ಬಿಡು|to give information for someone|Can I leave a message for her?
missed call|ತಪ್ಪಿದ ಕರೆ|a call you didn't answer|I saw your missed call.
you're on mute|ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮೈಕ್ ಆಫ್ ಆಗಿದೆ|we can't hear you on the call|Sorry, you're on mute.
can you hear me|ನನ್ನ ಮಾತು ಕೇಳಿಸುತ್ತಿದೆಯೇ|checking the call works|Hello, can you hear me?
you're breaking up|ನಿಮ್ಮ ಧ್ವನಿ ಕಡಿತಗೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಿದೆ|your voice is cutting|Sorry, you're breaking up.
share my screen|ನನ್ನ ಪರದೆ ಹಂಚಿಕೊ|to show your screen in a video call|Let me share my screen.
let's get started|ಶುರು ಮಾಡೋಣ|let's begin|Okay, let's get started.
to sum up|ಸಾರಾಂಶವಾಗಿ|to give the main points|To sum up, we need two more nurses.
any other business|ಇತರ ವಿಷಯಗಳು|other topics at the end of a meeting|Is there any other business?
action items|ಕ್ರಮದ ಅಂಶಗಳು|tasks to do after a meeting|Let's list the action items.
take notes|ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ ಮಾಡು|to write down points|Can someone take notes?
on the same page|ಒಂದೇ ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯದಲ್ಲಿ|having the same understanding|Let's make sure we're on the same page.
point of view|ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ|opinion|From my point of view, it's risky.
I'd like to add|ನಾನು ಸೇರಿಸಲು ಬಯಸುತ್ತೇನೆ|to add a point|I'd like to add one more point.
if I may|ಅನುಮತಿ ಇದ್ದರೆ|polite way to interrupt|If I may, I have a suggestion.
could you clarify|ಸ್ಪಷ್ಟಪಡಿಸುತ್ತೀರಾ|please explain more clearly|Could you clarify what you mean?
fair enough|ಸರಿಯೇ|that is reasonable|Fair enough, let's do it your way.
good point|ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಅಂಶ|a useful idea|That's a good point.
move on|ಮುಂದಿನ ವಿಷಯಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗು|to go to the next topic|Let's move on to the next point.
wrap up|ಮುಗಿಸು|to finish|Let's wrap up the meeting.
follow-up meeting|ಮುಂದುವರಿದ ಸಭೆ|another meeting later|We'll have a follow-up meeting on Friday.
conference call|ಕಾನ್ಫರೆನ್ಸ್ ಕರೆ|a call with many people|There's a conference call at ten.
working hours|ಕೆಲಸದ ಸಮಯ|the time you work|My working hours are 9 to 5.
weekly off|ವಾರದ ರಜೆ|a day off each week|Sunday is my weekly off.
half day|ಅರ್ಧ ದಿನ|working only part of the day|I'll take a half day tomorrow.
comp off|ಬದಲಿ ರಜೆ|a day off for working on a holiday|I'll take a comp off next week.
leave application|ರಜೆ ಅರ್ಜಿ|a request for leave|Submit your leave application online.
relieving letter|ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಪತ್ರ|a letter when you leave a job|I got my relieving letter.
experience certificate|ಅನುಭವ ಪ್ರಮಾಣಪತ್ರ|a letter showing your work years|Ask HR for an experience certificate.
joining date|ಸೇರುವ ದಿನಾಂಕ|the first day of a new job|My joining date is 1st December.
HR|ಮಾನವ ಸಂಪನ್ಮೂಲ ವಿಭಾಗ|human resources department|Contact HR about your salary.
onboarding|ಸೇರ್ಪಡೆ ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ|helping a new worker start|Onboarding takes one week.
orientation|ಪರಿಚಯ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮ|an introduction session|There's an orientation for new interns.
dress code|ಉಡುಪು ನಿಯಮ|rules about clothes at work|What's the dress code?
attendance register|ಹಾಜರಿ ಪುಸ್ತಕ|a book to sign when you come|Sign the attendance register.
biometric|ಬಯೋಮೆಟ್ರಿಕ್|fingerprint machine for attendance|Punch in at the biometric machine.
grievance|ಕುಂದುಕೊರತೆ|a formal complaint|Submit your grievance in writing.
harassment|ಕಿರುಕುಳ|unwanted bad behaviour|Report any harassment immediately.
work ethic|ಕೆಲಸದ ನೈತಿಕತೆ|attitude towards hard work|She has a strong work ethic.
multitask|ಏಕಕಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಹಲವು ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡು|to do many things at once|Nurses have to multitask.
deliver|ನೀಡು / ಪೂರೈಸು|to complete as promised|We delivered the project on time.
exceed expectations|ನಿರೀಕ್ಷೆಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಮಾಡು|to do better than expected|Her work exceeded expectations.
take responsibility|ಹೊಣೆ ಹೊರು|to accept a duty or blame|I take responsibility for the mistake.
learn from mistakes|ತಪ್ಪುಗಳಿಂದ ಕಲಿ|to improve after errors|Everyone makes mistakes; learn from them.
