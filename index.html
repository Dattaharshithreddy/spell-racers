How to play

🎮 Controls

🕹️Left stick: run. Drag the right side of the screen to turn the camera.
🗯️Word power: your real weapon. You see a Kannada word; tap (or say) the right English word to release a word blast that hurts every villain around you and heals you. Saying it out loud gives double power. Brutes and Mouna have shields that only word power breaks well.
🔫Fire (hold): shoots the nearest villain automatically. Good against small goons, weak against shields.
⤒Jump. Bangara can jump twice in the air.
⚡Dash (Bangara): zoom forward and can't be hit for a moment.
💥Pound (Gundamma): smash the ground and hurt every villain around you.
🤝Team-up: stand next to each other and Gundamma throws Bangara sky-high. You can land on rooftops!
🔁Swap heroes (solo). Your partner follows you and fights by your side.
🛺Drive: walk up to an auto-rickshaw, bike or jeep and tap the vehicle button. Stick = steer and accelerate, pull back to brake/reverse, ⤒ = horn. Your partner rides along and can shoot from the back. Run villains over!
🎙️Voice chat (two phones): tap 🎙️ at the top to talk to your friend. It switches off by itself while you do a speaking challenge.
💬Talk appears when you're next to the silent friend.
⌨️Keyboard: WASD move · Space jump/horn · F fire · R word power · Shift special · Q team-up · Tab swap · V vehicle · E talk · M map

📖 Each chapter

  1. Follow the golden light and the arrow at the top of the screen to the landmark.
  2. Find 6 glowing words. Villains with a 💬 bubble carry stolen words, so beat them to free the words. One word is always up high: use the team-up throw.
  3. Watch your ❤️ health. Eat 🟨 Mysore Pak dropped by villains to heal. If you're knocked out, you get back up near the landmark.
  4. Help two townspeople with a 💬 bubble. Each one has an everyday conversation (taking an auto, ordering food, asking the way): pick the right English reply, then say it out loud. You get 3 new words each time.
  5. Talk to the silent friend, put the words back into the story, then climb the speaking ladder: two words, a phrase, then the whole sentence. Colour comes back to the whole district!
  6. Old words come back in word powers and in 🧠 Daily practice, so you don't forget them. Open 🍱 My words to see your progress.
  7. After a chapter, 🌟 festival words appear in restored towns every day. The Mute Gang guards them.
