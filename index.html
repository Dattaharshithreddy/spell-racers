    How to play · 1/4

    🐰💨 JUMP! 🚧

    Tap anywhere to jump

    Tap again in the air for a double jump. Anything with a red JUMP! tag is an obstacle.

    Purple STAY LOW means birds flying high: don't jump. Blue SLOW is only a puddle.

    RUSH

    Collect letter coins

    Your three cards at the bottom are power words. Gold coins are the next letters they need. Fill a card and it glows.

    ⚡ RUSH · 🙈 AWKWARD

    Tap a glowing card

    Every word does what it means. RUSH makes you hurry. AWKWARD makes your rival trip. CONFIDENT gives you a shield.

    🚪 → 🪔🪔🪔

    Gates and Yaksharaj

    At a Word Gate the race pauses. Take your time and pick the right word. At the end, Yaksharaj gives you three trials: a riddle, a sentence to build and a sentence to say out loud.
