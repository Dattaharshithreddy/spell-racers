How to play · 1/5

TAP▲ JUMP

Tap to jump

Tap anywhere to jump. Tap again in the air for a double jump. Hop over anything with a red JUMP tag.

SWIPE ↓▼ SLIDE

Swipe down to slide

Purple SLIDE tags mean something is hanging low. Swipe down to slide under it. In the air, a swipe drops you down fast.

RUSH

Spell power words

Your three cards are power words. Gold letters are the ones they need next. Fill a card and it glows.

⚡ RUSH · 🪽 SOAR · 🙈 AWKWARD

Cast the word

Every word does what it means. RUSH speeds you up, SOAR lifts you into the sky, and AWKWARD trips the racer ahead of you.
