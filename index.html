How to play

  • Move with the left stick. Drag the right side of the screen to look around.
  • Glowing word orbs hide near each landmark. Touch one to learn the word. It goes into Gundamma's tiffin.
  • Wisps (Mouna's shadows) steal words. Bangara can ⚡ Dash through them; Gundamma can 💥 Pound the ground.
  • Some orbs float high up. Stand next to Gundamma and press 🤝 Throw to launch Bangara into the sky.
  • With all six words, talk to the silent friend, put the words back into the story, then say the sentence out loud. Colour returns!
  • After a chapter, 🌟 festival words appear in restored towns, so there's always something new to learn.
